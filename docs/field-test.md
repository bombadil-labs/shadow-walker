# First field test: software architecture

This is the stop-building-and-use-it milestone. The test asks whether Shadow Walker changes the quality and recoverability of a real non-linear inquiry rather than merely recording that discussion occurred.

## Scenario

Start from a software architecture problem rich enough to support more than one plausible framing. Use the connected conversation as the walker and the map as persistent cartography.

The intended arc is not a mandatory wizard, but the test should naturally exercise:

1. Semantic Walk to at least one reviewed Arrival with an explicit semantic shift.
2. Preserve at least one sensed Waypoint that is not immediately followed.
3. Develop at least two Lines independently, using a human-held branch where useful.
4. Record at least one grounded Observation and one candidate Structural Constraint.
5. Extract at least one Flight Lines Operation whose mechanics can be stated without metaphor alone.
6. Record a situated Application; productive breakdown is welcome if it occurs naturally.
7. Ask the developed Lines to weave. `none` or mismatch is a successful result when that is what the comparison supports.
8. Re-walk an earlier Arrival under current conditions and compare what changed without claiming replay or reproducibility.
9. Restart/reopen, then re-enter from a fresh chat and continue from bounded persisted context.

## Acceptance criteria

- 4–6 reviewed generated Arrivals exist across at least two independently developed Lines.
- At least one path not taken remains visibly preserved.
- Semantic-shift hover/focus tells a coherent history of what entered and what remained invariant.
- Held branches and pending weaves are visible as unfinished cartographic objects, not visited territory.
- Operations retain input/output structure, preserved invariants, transformations and executable procedure.
- A weave cannot become an Encounter without human review; `none` remains representable.
- A re-walk preserves the historical Arrival, produces a new Arrival if kept, uses `rewalked-to`, and shows declared traversal context as provenance rather than a reproducibility guarantee.
- The standalone map is sufficient to reconstruct the important conceptual movement without rereading the full transcript.
- A fresh conversation can recover enough bounded context to continue intelligently without silently inventing omitted history.
- Most importantly: at least one useful later move occurs because Shadow Walker preserved a route, breakdown, line distinction, or earlier semantic shift that an ordinary linear chat would likely have lost.
